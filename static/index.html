01 / why QuickTab

Everything a group trip needs. Nothing it doesn't.

01
No sign-up

One link in the WhatsApp group. Everyone picks their name and they're in.

02
UPI-native

A Pay button and a QR code with the amount already filled in.

03
Fewest payments

Debts are simplified: n people settle in at most n − 1 payments.

04
Private

UPI IDs are encrypted. Optional passwords, one per person.

05
Self-destructing

Open for 48 hours, read-only for a day, then deleted for good.

06
Share the receipt

Download a summary image or a CSV of every expense.
