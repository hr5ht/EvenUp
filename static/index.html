03 / questions

Good to know.

Still unsure? Open the live demo and click around. Nothing you do there is real.

Is it really free?

Yes. No fees, no ads, no premium plan. QuickTab only does the maths; the money moves app to app over UPI.

Does money pass through QuickTab?

Never. Pay opens your own UPI app with the amount and UPI ID filled in. You approve it there like any other payment.

Do my friends need an account?

No. They open the link, pick their name (or add it) and they're in. If you turned on passwords, they create one the first time.

Can someone pretend to be me?

Turn on Password protect when you open the tab. Then everyone, you included, signs in with their own password.

What happens after 48 hours?

The tab closes and turns read-only for a day so you can download the summary image or CSV. Then it's deleted for good.

Is my UPI ID safe?

UPI IDs are encrypted before they're stored, and only people with the tab link can see them.
